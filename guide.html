Budgeting Guide

The 50/30/20 rule, made simple.

Divide every peso you receive into three groups. It's one of the easiest ways to start budgeting, and it works for a ₱3,000 allowance or a ₱30,000 salary.

50%

Needs

  • Food and groceries
  • Transportation / fare
  • Rent, bills, and load
  • School supplies
30%

Wants

  • Eating out and milk tea
  • Movies, games, hobbies
  • New clothes and gadgets
  • Hangouts with friends
20%

Savings

  • Emergency fund
  • Future goals (tuition, laptop)
  • Paying off any debt
  • Bank or e-wallet savings

Know where your money goes

Four things every budget should keep an eye on.

Daily spend

Small daily costs like fare, snacks, and load add up quickly. Set a daily limit and stick to it.

Impulse buys

Saw a sale online? Wait 24 hours before checking out. If you still need it tomorrow, then buy it.

Upcoming expenses

Birthdays, projects, and school events are not surprises. Set aside a little each week for them.

Emergency fund

Aim to save at least 3 months of your needs for sudden costs like medicine or repairs.

Build your budget in 5 steps

You can finish this in less than 15 minutes, with just paper and a pen.

  1. Know your income. Write down your total monthly allowance or salary.
  2. List your needs. Add up fixed costs like fare, food, rent, and bills.
  3. Apply 50/30/20. Split your income into needs, wants, and savings.
  4. Save first. Move your 20% to savings as soon as you get your money.
  5. Review weekly. Check your spending every week and adjust if needed.

Sample monthly budget

Here's how a ₱10,000 monthly allowance looks using the 50/30/20 rule.

Example: ₱10,000 per month
Category Share Amount Example uses
Needs 50% ₱5,000 Meals, fare, load, school supplies
Wants 30% ₱3,000 Coffee, movies, hangouts, games
Savings 20% ₱2,000 Emergency fund, future goals
Total 100% ₱10,000
Tips
  • Bring baon or cook at home. Food is often the biggest leak in a student budget.
  • Keep savings in a separate account or e-wallet so you're not tempted to spend it.
  • Use cash envelopes for wants: when the envelope is empty, you're done for the month.
  • Treat yourself sometimes. A budget you enjoy is a budget you'll actually follow.
