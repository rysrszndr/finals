About Us

Making money talk less scary.

PiggyWise started as our CC 100 final project at Batangas State University. Many of us get our first allowance or salary without ever learning how to manage it, so we built a simple guide we wish we had: clear, friendly, and made for Filipino students and young adults.

Mission

To teach students and young adults simple, practical budgeting habits that help them spend wisely, save consistently, and feel in control of their money.

Vision

A generation of financially confident Filipinos who plan before they spend and who are always ready for life's surprises.
